# FyndEt — Cross-industry benchmarks

EmailOSINT is only one example. Evaluate the best relevant capabilities from identity security, digital risk protection, threat exposure management, breach monitoring, fraud prevention, public-web research, customer-facing security tools, premium SaaS, and enterprise investigation workflows.

## Public product references

- [Flare](https://flare.io/cyber-threat-intelligence-platform): identity exposure, alert context and remediation workflows.
- [Constella](https://constella.ai/): identity risk intelligence and digital risk protection.
- [Have I Been Pwned](https://haveibeenpwned.com/API/v3): consented or authorised breach notification and verified-domain services.
- [Flare documentation](https://docs.flare.io/): alerts, identifiers, investigation and team workflows.

These are independent benchmarking references and are not providers connected to FyndEt.

## Ambition

Assess each domain independently: breadth of licensed sources; verifiable email ownership; authorised account associations; digital risk; breach history; public indexed documents; historical traces; source freshness; evidence confidence; correlation; continuous monitoring; alerting; case management; reports; enterprise controls; premium mobile usability; subscription workflows; cPanel operation; SEO and AI-search discoverability.

## Honest maturity labels

Only rate a FyndEt capability Advanced when real integration, authorised access, coverage tests, reliability, mobile functionality and evidence provenance are demonstrated. An adapter in the repository is not a live connection. Benchmarking does not grant the right to collect or resell a provider's data.
